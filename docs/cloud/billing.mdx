# Billing

Owners and admins manage payment in **Workspace settings → Plan and billing**. Members can see the plan but cannot change it.

## Upgrade

Choose **Upgrade monthly** or **Upgrade yearly**. Dagr opens Mollie in your browser. Complete payment there.

The first payment sets up a mandate. After Mollie confirms, the workspace becomes Pro.

## Seats

The amount follows the number of home members. When someone joins or leaves as a home member, the subscription amount updates. External guests do not change the price. See [Plans](/cloud/plans).

## Cancel or resume

**Cancel Pro** keeps Pro until the end of the current period. After that the workspace returns to Free and 90-day history starts.

**Resume Pro** undoes a cancellation if the period has not ended yet.

## If checkout does not open

Allow the app to open your browser. Finish the Mollie page; closing it early leaves the workspace on Free.
