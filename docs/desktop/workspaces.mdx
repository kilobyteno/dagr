# Workspaces

A workspace is your team. It holds channels, people, and (on Cloud) the plan. The icons on the far left switch between workspaces.

## Create a workspace

New accounts start empty. Choose **Create workspace**, give it a name, and Dagr adds a `#general` channel for you.

Later, use the dashed plus on the left rail (**Add workspace**).

## Invite people

Open the workspace name at the top of the channel list, then **Invite people**. Enter an email.

- If that person already has an account on this server, they are added straight away.
- If not, you get an invite link to copy and send.

They can also paste the token from that link via the channel menu item **Accept invite…**.

## People and roles

**Workspace settings → People** lists members and pending invites.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Member | Use channels they can see, send messages |
| Admin | Invite, change roles, manage channels and workspace settings |
| Owner | Everything an admin can do, plus transfer ownership and delete the workspace |

People invited from another workspace on the same server show as **External**. They are guests, not home members.

Owners and admins can change a role, **Remove** someone, or **Revoke** a pending invite. **Leave** is for you. The last owner must transfer ownership before leaving.

## Workspace settings

Open the workspace name, then **Workspace settings**.

**General** is the name and icon (PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF up to 2 MB). Only owners and admins can change these.

**Verified domains** (owners and admins) lets you prove you own an email domain with a DNS TXT record. Turn on **Auto-join on signup** if new accounts with that email domain should join as members.

**Danger zone** deletes the workspace and its channels. Type the workspace name to confirm. This cannot be undone.

On Dagr Cloud, **Plan and billing** is also here. See [Plans](/cloud/plans) and [Billing](/cloud/billing). That section is hidden on a self-hosted server.
