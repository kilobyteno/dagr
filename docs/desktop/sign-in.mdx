# Sign in

The first screen is **Log in**. If you do not have an account yet, choose **Create an account**.

## Choose a server

Above the form, **Server** has two tabs.

**Cloud** connects to hosted Dagr. Use this if you are joining Dagr Cloud, not a server you run yourself.

**Self-hosted** asks for your API address. Type the URL of the Dagr server you (or your team) run, for example `http://localhost:8080` on your machine, or the HTTPS address you were given. The app checks that it can reach Dagr before you continue.

If the check fails, confirm the URL and that the server is running. See [Hosting](/hosting) if you are the person who set that server up.

## Create an account

You will be asked for a display name, email, password, and password confirmation. Password rules come from the server (length and character types).

After **Sign up**, you land in the app. New accounts do not have a workspace yet. Create one from [Workspaces](/desktop/workspaces).

## Verify your email

A banner may ask you to **Verify your email address**. Open the link in the message (it opens a short page on your server), or choose **Resend email**.

If you run the server, mail is off until you enable it. See [Email](/hosting/email).

You can also resend from your avatar menu, or from **Edit profile**. Until you verify, you can still use the app.

Password reset is not available yet. **Forgot password?** will say so.

## Sign out

Open your avatar at the bottom of the left rail and choose **Sign out**.

To stay signed in to this account and add another, see [Preferences](/desktop/preferences).
