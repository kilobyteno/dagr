# Preferences

These are the things that belong to you, or to this copy of the app, not to a workspace.

## Profile

Open your avatar (bottom of the left rail) → **Edit profile**.

You can change your display name, profile picture, and [default notifications](/desktop/notifications). Email is shown but not edited here.

Your `@handle` appears on messages and in member lists. It is set per workspace (how you look in that team), not in this dialog.

## Status

**Set a status** (or **Update status**) adds an emoji and a short note next to your name, for example “In a meeting”.

**Clear after** can be 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, a custom end time, or **Don't clear**. **Clear status** removes it now.

## App settings

The gear on the left rail opens settings for this device.

**Appearance:** Light, Dark, or System.

**Media:** **GIFs on hover only** keeps GIFs still until you move the pointer over them.

**Trusted domains:** links from these domains open without the “Open external link?” prompt. Verified workspace domains are trusted automatically. You can **Add domain** for others on this device.

## More than one account

Your avatar menu has **Accounts**.

**Add another account** opens sign in again without signing you out. Use this for a second server, or a second person on the same server.

The workspace rail and notification inbox include every account you have added. The active account is marked in the **Accounts** list.

**Remove this account** signs out of one account only. **Sign out** leaves the session you are using.
